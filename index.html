ABOUT ME

I feel very happy and lucky to work with my supervisors: Dr. Muhammad Waseem, Prof. Pekka Abrahamsson, and Prof. Jaakko Peltonen. My current research looks at failure patterns in agentic systems. I'm especially interested in attributing and explaining the failed outputs of AI agents: why they go wrong, where, and who's responsible.

Previously, I focused on Mutual Adversarial Learning, GNNs and GANs. I earned my M.Sc. in Computer Science at Hunan Normal University, Changsha, China, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Lianming Zhang.

Outside research, I love music & playing tennis, watching movies, and writing — in my spare time I write my own songs. And yes, I'm also a lifelong gaming fan.
